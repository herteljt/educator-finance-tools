The % of your next earned dollar that goes to taxes. Most college students: 10–22%. Unsure? Leave it at 12%.
Why does tax rate matter? Wages are taxed; scholarship money generally isn't (when used for tuition and fees). A $1,000 scholarship is worth the same as roughly $1,136 in wages at a 12% tax rate — because you'd owe about $136 in taxes on the wages but nothing on the scholarship.
