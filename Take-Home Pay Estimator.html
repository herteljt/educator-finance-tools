STEP 1

Your Pay

Enter your gross salary: the yearly amount in your contract, before anything is taken out. This tool uses 2026 tax rules for Wisconsin educators.

Many districts let you choose 10-month or 12-month pay. Your yearly total is the same either way. Only the size of each paycheck changes.
STEP 2

Pension and Health Insurance

WRS is the Wisconsin Retirement System, the state pension. It is required. The 2026 employee rate is 7.2% (7.25% in 2027).
Find this on your pay stub. If your district takes 12 months of premiums from 10 paychecks, enter the larger per-check amount.
STEP 3

Optional: FSA and Supplemental Retirement

Pre-tax money for medical costs. 2026 limit: $3,400. Many plans are use-it-or-lose-it.
Only for child care or dependent adult care. 2026 limit: $7,500.
Supplemental retirement savings (403(b) or 457(b))

Optional accounts offered through many districts, in addition to WRS. Traditional comes out before income tax, lowering your tax bill now. Roth comes out after tax and can be withdrawn tax-free in retirement.

2026 limit: $24,500 per year for a 403(b), and a separate $24,500 for a 457(b).
STEP 4

Optional: Other Deductions

Add anything else taken from your paycheck, such as dental, vision, union dues, or life insurance. Enter the amount per paycheck. Dental and vision are usually Before tax. Union dues and most life insurance are After tax. If unsure, choose After tax.

Your Estimate

About This Estimate

This is a planning estimate, not tax advice. It uses 2026 federal and Wisconsin rules and your yearly totals, so actual withholding will differ depending on your W-4 and WT-4. It does not include:
  • Tax credits (child tax credit, earned income credit, saver’s credit, and others)
  • Itemized deductions, or income other than this salary (a spouse’s pay, a side job)
  • Student loan interest, HSA contributions, stipends, or extra-duty pay

Your pay stub is the final word. Compare this estimate against a recent stub.
